# Botanical art v2

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. The chosen project asset is `src/assets/botanical-growth-atlas-v2.png`. The photographic search reference was used to identify a botanical direction; no source photograph is bundled in the game.

## Final generation prompt

Create a game-ready transparent PNG sprite atlas, square format. EXACT 4 by 4 equal-cell grid, 16 individual cannabis specimens, no text or drawn grid. All sprites isolated with real transparent alpha channel, not a drawn checkerboard. Wide spacing: no sprite larger than 60% of its own cell's width or height, and all roots at 80% down their own cell. Nothing touches cell edges. Photorealistic botanical cutout style, realistic dense overlapping serrated fan leaves with detailed venation, organic irregular branching and natural elongated green frosty buds with pale resin and copper pistils. Not cartoon, not flat illustration. NO bright color fringes. Row1: cracked brown seed; seed with short white root; small sprout; cotyledon seedling. Row2: first serrated leaf pair plant; young leafy plant; branching plant; bushy vegetative plant. Row3: first flowers; small green developing buds; swelling buds; mature full flowering cannabis plant. Row4: late flowering plant; broad mature plant; narrow tall mature plant; wilted mature plant. Same recognizable plant genetics throughout. Roots only on early plants; stem base only for larger plants. Full plants, no pot, no soil, no shadows, no background. Rich true green, muted highlights. Every specimen is centered inside its own generously padded square, with complete uninterrupted clear transparent separation between all sixteen subjects. Botanical product photography quality.

## Integration

The delivered atlas is 1254 square pixels. Source gutters and alpha bounds are measured at load time. Frames are root-aligned and size-interpolated; stress and persistent damage are visual treatments over the preserved source art. Render version 1 remains available for archived records. Review the artwork with the development server at `/plant-art-review.html`.
